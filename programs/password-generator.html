How strength is measured: “bits” counts how many guesses a random search would need (2bits). The time shown assumes an attacker making 100 billion guesses a second against a stolen, fast-hashed database, which is a pessimistic case. Most accounts are fine at 60+ bits; use 80+ for anything that protects other passwords, such as your password manager.
